Senior Brand & Visual Designer · São Paulo

I'm a Brazilian Senior Brand and Visual Designer based in São Paulo, working globally with brands of different sizes and industries.

I was born in a small countryside town. I've always been drawn to drawings, comic books, magazines and illustration. From an early age, I was the one solving every computer issue for family and friends. Curiosity came before the profession, over time, it became a method.

I see design as problem solving, not as something purely aesthetic. At the same time, I bring a strong sense of sensitivity into my work, using it to add emotion and humanity to each project. I focus on understanding the context, the business and the people involved, because when a brand or visual system is thoughtfully built, it creates clarity, trust and stronger relationships.

After years working in branding-focused design agencies of different scales, I chose to pursue an independent path to deepen processes, decisions and creative standards.

My work combines strategy, in-depth research and aesthetic sensitivity. I translate concepts into cohesive visual systems designed to sustain a brand over time and across multiple touchpoints.

I'm drawn to projects that push me beyond my comfort zone. From beauty to woodworking, from digital platforms to craft-driven products, moving across industries and cultures expands my perspective and shapes everything I create.
