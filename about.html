About me

I'm a Brazilian Senior Brand and Visual Designer based in São Paulo, working globally with brands of different sizes and industries.

I was born in a small countryside town. I've always been drawn to drawings, comic books, magazines and illustration. Curiosity came before the profession; over time, it became a method.

I see design as problem solving, not as something purely aesthetic. At the same time, I bring a strong sense of sensitivity into my work, using it to add emotion and humanity to each project.

After years working in branding agencies of different scales, I chose to pursue an independent path to deepen processes, decisions and creative standards.

My work combines strategy, in-depth research and aesthetic sensitivity. I translate concepts into cohesive visual systems designed to sustain a brand over time.
