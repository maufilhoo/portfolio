About

Senior Brand & Visual Designer, São Paulo.

Nice to meet you, I’m Mauricio Filho, an independent brand designer and art director based in São Paulo.

I grew up in a small town in the countryside of São Paulo, drawing, collecting magazines and comic books, and paying attention to everything around me. I still work the same way. Before looking at the market, I look for the story a brand tells without having to explain itself: where it comes from, what it believes in, the detail nobody else noticed. That’s where the best ideas come from. I turn those findings into expressive attributes and explore what each one can say through form, image, color, motion and experience. My process runs deep because I’m after truth, not trends.

From there, I blend sensitivity and strategy to build systems that live beyond the static. I move just as naturally between craft and AI, from type design to motion and generative tools. Bold ideas, always with a reason behind them.

For over 9 years, I’ve worked with branding agencies and consultancies. I was a Senior Designer at Tátil Design and have collaborated with R/GA, FutureBrand and Lysch on brands such as Itaú, Caixa, Natura, Enjoei, 99 and Google.

Going independent was a deliberate choice to broaden my creative range. Along the way, I’ve moved across different challenges and contexts, from hands-on execution to the creative direction of teams and projects. I know the process inside out, from briefing to type production, from logo to photo shoot, and to me the final visual is just one part of it. What truly excites me is the legacy: a brand that keeps making sense long after launch.

I’m especially drawn to digital projects where craft can take a brand’s identity beyond the obvious. If you’re not afraid to take risks and enjoy challenging the market, I’d love to build it together.

About me

Nice to meet you, I'm Mauricio Filho, a Brazilian multidisciplinary designer and art director based in São Paulo. With a focus on branding and visual identity, I've worked with brands of different sizes and industries across Brazil and internationally.

I grew up in a small town in the countryside of São Paulo. Drawing, collecting magazines and comic books, and paying attention to everything around me were part of my everyday life. Long before I thought of design as a career, curiosity had already shaped the way I saw the world.

Today, I see design as a way to solve problems and create meaning. Aesthetics matter, but they should always be driven by intention. I enjoy balancing strategic thinking with sensitivity to create brands that feel human, distinctive and memorable. I'm especially drawn to projects that aren't afraid to embrace a strong personality.

Research and concept development are my favorite parts of the process. After several years working at branding agencies of different sizes and profiles, I chose to become an independent designer so I could be more involved in key decisions, dive deeper into the process, and keep expanding my creative perspective.

My work combines strategy, in-depth research and visual direction to turn ideas into cohesive brand systems designed to evolve and stay relevant over time.
