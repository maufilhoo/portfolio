Welcome

Mauricio Filho

Brand & Visual Designer
based in São Paulo.

Multidisciplinary Designer
focused on brand & digital.

Currently Independent. Formerly Senior Designer at Tátil Design.

Collaborated with agencies and consultancies like R/GA, FutureBrand, Monigle (US), Marcas com Sal, among others. 9+ years building logos, visual identities, typography, iconography, packaging, signage, and campaign art direction for brands like Itaú, Ambev, Natura, Avon, Google, Danone, C6, Caixa, Enjoei, Vibra and 99 App.

Recognized at the Brazilian Graphic Design Biennial, Latin American Design Awards (LAD) and Brazil Design Awards (BDA).
